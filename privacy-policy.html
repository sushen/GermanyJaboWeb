4. Authentication & Guest Mode

The application provides flexible access options to accommodate learners:

Email / Password Authentication

When you register with an email address and password, Firebase Authentication securely manages your user credentials. Passwords are password-hashed server-side by Firebase and are never stored in plain text or accessible by us.

Google Sign-In

If you choose to sign in using Google Sign-In, Firebase Authentication processes basic profile information made available by Google (such as your name, email address, and account ID). We do not request or receive full access to your Google account or Google Drive.

Guest Mode

Users may access the application using Guest Learner Mode without registering or creating a persistent server-side account. In Guest Mode, a temporary session identifier is generated locally, and learning progress is stored solely on your local device.

7. Local Device Storage

The application utilizes local storage mechanisms on your device:

  • Android Application: Uses Android SharedPreferences and DataStore to store lesson completion state, quiz scores, sound preferences, and session tokens.
  • Web Application: Uses browser localStorage (key: germana1_user_data_v1) to store offline chapter progress and preference settings.

Data stored in local device storage remains on your device and is not transmitted to external servers. You can clear this data at any time through your Android app settings ("Clear Data") or browser settings.

8. Course Progress Handling

All learning progress—including completed chapters, exercise test scores, and last visited sections—is stored locally on your device.

Course progress is NOT synchronized across devices or stored on a remote cloud database. If you switch devices or uninstall the application, your local progress will be reset unless restored via local device backup mechanisms.

10. Data Retention

We adhere to minimal data retention practices:

  • Account Information: Retained in Firebase Authentication as long as your user account remains active. Deleted upon receiving a validated account deletion request.

  • Local Course Progress: Saved on your device indefinitely until manually cleared or app uninstallation.
  • Advertising & Diagnostic Data: Retained by Google AdMob according to Google's standard data retention schedules.

13. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration:

Please note that no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure, and absolute security cannot be guaranteed.
